# Campus Cats agent instructions

## App Store submissions and marketing assets

- Before answering App Store Connect submission questions or drafting listing metadata, read [marketing/app-store-listing.md](marketing/app-store-listing.md). Use its maintained field answers, review instructions, privacy guidance, and accessibility guidance as the starting point instead of inventing replacement copy.
- For header, search-result, screenshot, or preview upload instructions, also read [marketing/app-store/README.md](marketing/app-store/README.md). Give exact upload files and placements, and preserve its distinctions between illustrative images, native screenshots, and video previews.
- Verify current build, deployment, and App Store Connect status separately. These documents provide guidance and draft copy; they do not prove that a build, backend change, or submission has shipped. Flag any conflict between the documents and verified current behavior before recommending an answer.
