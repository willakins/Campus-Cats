# Release notes

What's new in Campus Cats, with the newest version first.

## Version 1.3.1 - Draft

Make room for the details your club needs, explore sightings in your area, and enjoy
clearer record pages.

### Added

- **Your club's own fields:** club presidents can add extra information to cat profiles, sightings, and feeding stations through More → Additional Fields. Choose text, numbers, yes/no answers, or a list of options. Hide fields you no longer need and restore them later without losing saved information.
- **Public welcome page:** learn about Campus Cats and find your university from the website's welcome page.

### Improved

- **Sightings map:** the map loads sightings for the area and age filter you select. Use Load more to see additional reports.
- **Record details:** clearer spacing separates Additional information from the cards around it.

### Fixed

- Retrying a failed additional-information save keeps your latest edits without creating a duplicate record.
- Switching accounts or clubs no longer lets delayed map or additional-information updates bring back the previous view.
- App Store previews now use the familiar Cat-alog name consistently.

## Version 1.3.0

Performance and security improvements help you browse your community and keep
account and club information protected.

### Improved

- **Community:** alerts, events, surveys, and votes can appear as they become ready, even when another section takes longer to load.
- **Browsing:** reduced repeated loading when viewing sightings, cat profiles, and contributor information.
- **Member profiles:** more efficient loading of a member's imported sightings.

### Fixed

- Switching accounts, clubs, or roles clears the previous billing view and keeps payment links tied to the correct club.

### Security

- Strengthened account restrictions and club permissions.
- Added safeguards for club subscription, invoice, and payment information.
- Improved checks for invalid report details and links.
- Strengthened protections around Georgia Tech sign-in.

## Version 1.2.0

A refreshed look makes Campus Cats feel more modern and consistent.

### Improved

- Updated cards, spacing, and controls across sightings, feeding stations, the Cat-alog, and Community.
- Reorganized member profiles make achievements and favorite cats easier to browse.
- Refreshed welcome and university-selection screens help you find your community.

### Fixed

- University search results stay accessible while the keyboard is open.

## Version 1.1.0

Campus Cats grows beyond Georgia Tech to support university cat communities across
the country, with new ways to connect, organize, and support your club.

### Added

- **More campuses:** find your university and join your club's own community, with its branding, members, and cat records.
- **Club subscriptions:** presidents can manage subscriptions, payment methods, and invoices on the web. Members can view subscription status in the app.
- **Club chat:** share messages and reactions with fellow members. Officers can send club pings and moderate conversations.
- **Events and surveys:** keep up with club activities and share feedback through named or anonymous surveys.
- **Community voting:** take part in club contests and presidential elections, including nominations and private ballots.
- **Donation pages:** learn about your club's work and how to support it.

### Improved

- Alerts, events, surveys, voting, and chat are brought together in Community.
- Each club can manage its own membership and activities.

### Coming soon

- Direct in-app donation payments. This version provides donation information pages.

## Version 1.0.0

The initial capstone release brings Georgia Tech's Campus Cats club together to
report sightings, coordinate care, and get to know the campus cats.

### Added

- **Report a sighting:** share a cat's name, condition, location, and photos.
- **Explore the map:** find reported sightings and filter them by age.
- **Meet the cats:** browse photos, stories, and recent sightings in the Cat-alog.
- **Coordinate feeding stations:** find stations, check stock status, and record restocking updates.
- **Stay informed:** read club alerts and receive notifications for published updates.
- **Georgia Tech sign-in:** use your university account to access the app. Alumni can apply for access.
- **Club tools:** authorized administrators can manage cat profiles, stations, alerts, and membership.
- **Club information:** find contact details in Settings.

### Fixed

- Improved Android login and date selection, including access to the login button while the keyboard is open.
- Made report buttons more consistent and easier to tap.
- Added clearer messages when photo permissions prevent an upload.
- Prevented back navigation from returning signed-in members to the login screen.
- Restored the Android status bar.
- Prevented duplicate Cat-alog entries during uploads.

### Known limitations at launch

These describe the original release, rather than the current app.

- Georgia Tech sign-in could fail on the first attempt in some browser sessions; retrying could complete sign-in.
- Blocking a member did not always prevent them from signing in again.
- Tablet layouts were less polished than phone layouts.
- Clubs could not add their own information fields.
- The map loaded more sighting information than it needed.
